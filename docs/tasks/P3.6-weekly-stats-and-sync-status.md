## Goal
Useful aggregates computed in SQL, and a status endpoint that tells how fresh and healthy sync is.

## Scope
1. `GET /v1/stats/weekly?from=&to=&sport_type=` — per ISO week (`date_trunc('week', start_date AT TIME ZONE 'UTC')`,
   Monday start): `week_start`, `count`, `distance_m`, `moving_time_s`, `elevation_gain_m`;
   weeks with no activities inside the range are returned with zeros (`generate_series`).
   Excludes soft-deleted. Includes `last_synced_at`.
2. `GET /v1/sync/status` — last backfill and incremental run per athlete (status, finished_at,
   upserted), last webhook received/processed, pending/failed/dead-letter webhook counts,
   current rate-limit usage ratios from Redis, circuit breaker state.
3. A hand-computed fixture: `tests/fixtures/weekly_stats.json` (~20 activities over 4 weeks,
   including a week boundary at Sunday 23:30 UTC / Monday 00:30 UTC and an empty week).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Weekly stats match the hand-computed fixture exactly (integration test).
- [ ] Empty weeks are zero-filled; sport filter works (test).
- [ ] Sync status reflects a running backfill, a failed webhook and an open circuit (test with seeded state).
