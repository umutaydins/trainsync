## Goal
In savage mode the coach also goes after unhealthy choices: junk food, sugar, alcohol,
cigarettes and staying up late get a brutal roast of the choice plus one concrete move, and so do
high-sugar food logs. A second dial, the profanity level, decides whether only the roasts swear
(`roast`, as today) or every savage message does (`max`). Harsh stays harsh, harm stays out:
body talk, slurs, food punishment and restriction are never allowed, and pain, illness or
distress switch the coach to a calm reply in every mode.

## Context
- Intensity, the savage packs, rotation, punchlines, the local lines file and the guardrails
  (ADR 0030, P6.11); runtime language per chat in `chat_settings` (ADR 0031, P6.12).
- Food logging and Open Food Facts (ADR 0019, P6.8). Foods store kcal and macros per 100 g; sugar
  and the NOVA group are not stored yet.
- The old rule "food is never roasted: eat it, log it, stay in the daily target" (ADR 0030) is
  replaced here by the food rule below. Its other half stays: nothing pushes eating below the
  nutrition target.

## Scope
1. **Food rule (savage, tr and en).**
   - Chat triggers: eating or planning junk: sweets and sugar (baklava, dessert, chocolate,
     cake, candy, ice cream, cola and other soft drinks, energy drinks), fast food (burger, pizza,
     döner, fries, nuggets, …), fried food, alcohol, cigarettes and vaping, and staying up late.
   - Food-log triggers (`/eat`): an item with more than 22.5 g sugar per 100 g or Open Food Facts
     NOVA group 4 (threshold `HIGH_SUGAR_PER_100G`), and a free-sugar item that takes the day's
     free sugar past `SUGAR_LIMIT_G` (default 50). Free sugar (WHO): items in the sweets, desserts
     or sugary-drinks groups, or NOVA 4; fruit, vegetables and plain dairy never count and are
     never roasted for sugar (migration 0014: `food_group` on foods and logs, from OFF
     `categories_tags` or set by hand; the day summary gets `free_sugars_g`).
   - Reply: a savage `food_roast` line (heavy swearing, roasts the CHOICE), then exactly one
     concrete move for that kind of choice (smaller portion, a better swap, "log it now", phone
     away and lights out), then the day's target unchanged ("today's target stays N kcal").
   - New category `food_roast` in savage/tr and savage/en, at least 20 lines each.
   - Data: `foods` and `food_logs` get `sugars_100g` and `nova_group` (migration 0013; logs
     snapshot them like the macros); Open Food Facts fills them; the day summary gets `sugars_g`.
2. **Profanity level, separate from intensity.** `BOT_PROFANITY=roast|max` (default `roast`).
   - `roast`: today's behaviour: the savage lines swear, everything else is the tone pack.
   - `max` (only while savage is in effect): every message swears. Greetings, confirmations
     (`/eat`, `/weight`, set logged, `/lang`, `/swear`), reminders, check-in questions, workout
     start / rest / summary, weekly summary, PR praise, errors and the assistant, both languages.
     Savage lines come from the `max` variant of their category; every other message gets a
     rotated `max_opener` line in front of it.
   - Per chat at runtime, stored like `/lang` (`chat_settings.profanity`, migration 0012):
     `/swear` shows "max" / "roast" buttons, `/swear max` and `/swear roast` switch directly. No
     stored value: `BOT_PROFANITY`. Listed in `/help` (English, like every command).
   - Packs: every savage category gets `max` lines in both languages, at least as many as its
     roast lines (punchlines too); the local lines file takes a `max:` section per language and a
     top-level `punchline_en_max`.
   - Assistant: in max, the system prompt says to swear naturally in every reply, neutral
     answers and praise included; the hard limits still override it.
   - The worker reads the stored profanity at send time, like the language.
3. **Unknown commands** (e.g. `/harder`) get a reply in the current tone that points to `/help`:
   the tone pack's line, or a savage `unknown_command` line in savage mode.
4. **Guardrails override everything (each one tested).**
   - Roast the choice or the effort, never the body, weight or appearance; no slurs.
   - Never burn-it-off, punishment cardio, skipping meals or eating below the nutrition target
     (blocklist for every line and every assistant reply; the food moves are reviewed text).
   - Pain, injury, illness, and distress (guilt spiral, threw up, haven't eaten all day,
     self-hate, binge talk) → a calm reply from the neutral layer: no swearing (not even in max),
     no roast, no assistant call. Distress is checked before pain, pain before any trigger.

## Out of scope
Per-chat intensity (still `BOT_INTENSITY` plus `/yumusak`); swearing in normal intensity; stack
and uptime alerts (operations messages, not the coach); a sugar figure in the web dashboard; a
per-drink sugar threshold (OFF reports drinks per 100 ml, judged with the same 22.5 g).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A baklava message (tr and en) in savage gets a `food_roast` line and a swap / portion
      move with the unchanged day target.
- [x] A high-sugar `/eat` log (and a NOVA 4 one) gets a roast in savage; a plain one does not; a
      log that takes the day over `SUGAR_LIMIT_G` says so.
- [x] Only free sugar counts toward the limit: an apple and plain yoghurt never trigger, cola and
      baklava do.
- [x] Max applies to a confirmation (`/eat`), a reminder and PR praise; roast mode keeps those
      confirmations clean (no opener, no swear words).
- [x] `/swear` persists across a bot restart; default `BOT_PROFANITY`; the worker uses the stored
      value.
- [x] Distress and injury messages get the calm reply, without swearing, even in max.
- [x] Every pack has every category (roast and max, incl. `food_roast`, `unknown_command`,
      `profanity_set`, `max_opener`) in both languages, max ≥ roast per category; built-in lines
      pass the guardrail blocklist.
- [x] Unknown commands point to `/help` in normal and savage mode.
- [x] ADR 0032, ADR 0030 food rule replaced, `operations.md`, README, `.env` examples, compose,
      diagrams, OpenAPI and web types updated.
