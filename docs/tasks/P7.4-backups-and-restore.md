## Goal
Losing the Mac's disk must not mean losing years of training data.

## Scope
1. `backup` service (or LaunchAgent) runs nightly `pg_dump -Fc` to `BACKUP_DIR`
   (default an iCloud Drive folder), encrypted with `age` using a public key from env;
   retention 7 daily / 4 weekly / 6 monthly.
2. `scripts/restore-check.sh`: restores the latest backup into a scratch Postgres container and
   compares row counts of key tables with live; exits non-zero on mismatch.
3. Weekly restore-check scheduled; result reported to Telegram (P7.8).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Retention logic unit-tested; restore-check passes against a generated backup in CI.
