## Goal
The coach tells each user how their week and month went, without being asked, and on `/stats`.

## Context
ADR 0038. The bot reads `/v1/stats/summary` as the linked user (ADR 0036); the worker builds the
digest from the same service, in the user's language and coach voice (ADR 0016, 0030, 0032).

## Scope
1. `/stats [week|month|3m|6m|year|all]` (default `week`): a compact summary with the change
   against the previous period; gains are praised, drops roasted in the savage voice, and the
   guardrails hold (never body weight or appearance as a roast target).
2. A weekly digest every Monday and a monthly one on the 1st at `STATS_DIGEST_TIME` (the
   user's local time), for the period that just ended; quiet hours postpone it, and each digest
   is sent at most once (notifications table).
3. `STATS_DIGEST_ENABLED` (empty = on with a bot token) and a per-user switch in the settings.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `/stats month` answers with the month's numbers and changes in tr and en, in each tone.
- [ ] Monday 09:00 local sends last week's digest once per user, in two time zones; quiet hours
      hold it; the 1st sends the monthly one (tests with a fake clock).

## Verify
`make verify`
